02 Core capabilities

Quality issues on major projects are rarely caused by lack of procedures — they happen when systems don't translate into execution.

My work focuses on closing that execution gap across the entire program lifecycle, turning high-level compliance manuals into operational site discipline.

Governance paradigm Practical on-site implementation over theoretical binders
